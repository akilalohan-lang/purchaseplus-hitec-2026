## We Built a Machine That Generates Pipeline Before Sales Calls

Most B2B SaaS companies generate pipeline the same way they have for 20 years. Marketing runs campaigns. SDRs cold call. AEs demo. The cycle is long, expensive, and heavily dependent on individual performance.

PurchasePlus built something different. We call it the GTM Machine: a system of 14 AI agents running in parallel, each responsible for a specific part of the pre-sales process. Together, they identify, research, qualify, and engage hotel procurement prospects before a sales rep ever enters the conversation.

This is not marketing automation with a new label. It is a purpose-built AI system that replaced the manual top-of-funnel work with a scalable, data-driven process.

## What Is the GTM Machine?

The GTM Machine is an interconnected system of AI agents, each with a defined role in the prospect journey. Some agents are responsible for identifying potential customers based on firmographic signals. Others research the organizational structure of target accounts, mapping procurement and finance decision-makers. Others monitor for buying triggers, events that indicate a hotel group is in a window of procurement evaluation.

Each agent feeds its output into the next stage of the system. By the time a prospect reaches the pre-MQL threshold where a human sales rep would typically engage, the system has already built a detailed account profile, identified the most relevant decision-makers, determined the highest-probability entry points, and drafted an initial outreach sequence tailored to the specific organization.

The sales rep enters the conversation informed, not starting from scratch.

## The 14 Agents: Roles and Responsibilities

The GTM Machine includes agents across three operational layers:

**Discovery and qualification agents** handle market mapping, firmographic scoring, technographic research (identifying what systems a target hotel group already uses), and intent signal monitoring. These agents build the top of the pipeline and continuously update the target account list as hotel groups enter or leave buying windows.

**Research and enrichment agents** build detailed account profiles for qualified targets. This includes organizational mapping (who owns procurement, who owns finance, who has budget authority), recent news and trigger event monitoring, and competitive intelligence (what systems the group is currently using and when contracts are likely to expire).

**Engagement preparation agents** produce outreach assets tailored to each account. This includes personalized email sequences, LinkedIn connection messaging, and briefing documents for sales reps before their first call. The output is not generic. It references the specific organization, its portfolio, its apparent procurement challenges, and the PurchasePlus capabilities most relevant to their situation.

## From Anonymous Visitor to Qualified Lead

The GTM Machine does not only operate on outbound targets. It also works the inbound side.

When a hotel operator visits the PurchasePlus website, the system identifies the organization from IP and behavioral signals, enriches the profile with account data, determines which page engagement patterns indicate active evaluation, and routes the qualified signal to the appropriate sales rep with context already assembled.

A hotel group researching procure-to-pay solutions does not arrive at a PurchasePlus sales rep as a cold contact. They arrive as a warm, researched prospect with a detailed account brief already prepared.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the GTM Machine?**
The GTM Machine is a proprietary system of 14 AI agents developed by PurchasePlus to automate the top-of-funnel sales process. The agents identify hotel procurement prospects, research target accounts, monitor for buying triggers, and prepare personalized outreach, all before a human sales rep engages.

**How many AI agents does PurchasePlus run in its go-to-market system?**
PurchasePlus runs 14 AI agents in parallel within the GTM Machine, covering discovery, qualification, research, enrichment, and engagement preparation across its target markets.

**Does the GTM Machine replace the sales team?**
No. The GTM Machine handles the research and preparation work that would otherwise consume most of a sales rep's time. The sales team's focus shifts from sourcing and qualifying to having high-quality, informed conversations with prospects who are already researched and contextually ready for engagement.

**Is the GTM Machine available as a product for other companies?**
The GTM Machine is an internal PurchasePlus operational capability, not a product offering. It is described here as evidence of how deeply AI is integrated into the way PurchasePlus operates, not just what the platform does for customers.

**Can hotel procurement buyers benefit from understanding how AI is used in sales?**
Yes. Hotels evaluating technology vendors should ask how any vendor uses AI in their own operations. A company that sells AI-powered procurement software but does not use AI in its own operations is a different proposition than one that runs its entire go-to-market on AI-native infrastructure.
