# Strattam Capital Invests in PurchasePlus: AI-Forward Hotel Procurement Platform Enters North America

**META:** PurchasePlus secures a majority investment from Strattam Capital to accelerate its US market entry. The AI-forward procure-to-pay platform trusted by Accor, IHG, and Marriott debuts in North America at HITEC 2026, Booth 3715, San Antonio, June 15-18.

## A 25-Year Platform Enters North America

PurchasePlus (Marketboomer Holdings Pty Ltd) has announced a majority investment from Strattam Capital, a private equity firm with over $1 billion in assets under management and more than 50 partnerships with founder-led B2B software companies since 2014. The investment will accelerate PurchasePlus's entry into the US market and deepen its AI-driven product capabilities.

Strattam Capital is headquartered in Austin and San Francisco. The firm has a specific focus on founder-led software businesses in supply chain, procurement, and adjacent verticals. It brings both capital and hands-on operational expertise to its portfolio companies.

## What the Investment Funds

The Strattam Capital partnership follows the firm's Five-Point Plan process, which aligns investors with founders and CEOs before signing to ensure shared vision and execution clarity. For PurchasePlus, this means capital, US market access, and a partner who has invested in supply chain and procurement software before.

Malcolm Jull, CEO of PurchasePlus, said: "Strattam's deep expertise in supply chain and hospitality-adjacent software and their track record of helping businesses scale globally makes them exactly the right partner for this next chapter. With our alignment on the Five-Point Plan, we have a clear path and the resources to bring PurchasePlus to the US and cement our position as the gold standard for forward focused hospitality procurement worldwide."

The investment follows a period of significant product transformation. PurchasePlus rebuilt its platform architecture for the AI era while retaining over two decades of hospitality-specific domain knowledge.

Jull added: "We've taken our many years of domain expertise and rebuilt our business model for the fast-changing needs of both buyers and suppliers, providing a legacy understanding with modern solutions that hotel operators and staff can readily use, love and greatly benefit from."

## What PurchasePlus Does

PurchasePlus is an AI-forward procure-to-pay platform purpose-built for the global hospitality industry. The platform touches 100% of purchasing, automating invoice processing, purchase ordering, inventory management, and supplier connectivity within a single environment.

Key platform capabilities include:

- AI invoice automation with 99% accuracy and 90% straight-through processing
- Real-time commitment-based budgeting with live GP% visibility before invoices arrive
- Multi-property rollup for F&B performance across hotel portfolios
- A supplier network built over 25 years, connecting hotels directly to verified vendors

The platform is trusted by Accor, IHG Hotels and Resorts, Marriott, Capella Hotels, and Landmark Group, across more than 40 million invoices processed.

## Why Strattam Chose PurchasePlus

Hilary Fleischer, Partner at Strattam Capital, said: "PurchasePlus is exactly the kind of investment we built Strattam to make. Malcolm and his team have built a mission-critical, AI-forward product in a vertical where deep domain expertise and high-touch customer service are genuine differentiators. Our prior investments in supply chain, procurement, and hospitality-adjacent software give us privileged insights into what it takes to win in this market. We couldn't be more excited to partner with Malcolm and the entire PurchasePlus team as they bring their platform to the world."

Mitch Hulst, COO of PurchasePlus, added: "Really looking forward to working with such a capable investment partner to deliver even more value to our customers."
