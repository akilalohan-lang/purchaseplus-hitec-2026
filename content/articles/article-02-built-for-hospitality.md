## Hotels Are Not Like Any Other Business

Ask a hotel GM what keeps them up at night and purchasing rarely tops the list. That is exactly the problem.

Hotel purchasing happens everywhere, every day, across every department. A chef ordering produce at 6am. A housekeeping supervisor reordering amenities at midnight. A maintenance team buying parts for a repair that cannot wait. And somewhere in the back office, an accounts payable team handling hundreds of invoices from dozens of suppliers, often still manually.

The hospitality industry has one of the most complex purchasing environments in the world. Multiple cost centres. Multiple suppliers. Multiple departments with purchasing authority. And real-time pressure to maintain service standards while controlling costs.

A generic ERP does not understand this. A procurement tool built for manufacturing does not map to it. A spreadsheet cannot scale across it.

This is why PurchasePlus has been building exclusively for hospitality since 1999.

## What 25 Years in the Room Taught Us

Twenty-five years of working inside hotels teaches you things no product roadmap session will surface.

You learn that hotel operators do not want more technology. They want less friction. A chef should not need a procurement course to raise a purchase order. A finance director should not wait until month-end to see whether F&B is on track.

You learn that supplier relationships are everything. The supplier network in hospitality is specific, local, and built on trust. Plugging any new platform into that network from scratch takes years. PurchasePlus has been building supplier relationships for over two decades, which means hotels that join the platform connect to a verified network immediately rather than starting from zero.

You learn that the data is the product. After 40 million invoices, the platform knows what a fair price looks like for a kilogram of beef tenderloin in a five-star hotel. It knows which suppliers have the tightest delivery windows. It knows where maverick spending happens and why.

That institutional knowledge is embedded in the platform, not bolted on top of it.

## The Platform We Rebuilt for the AI Era

For most of our history, PurchasePlus was a best-in-class operations tool. It streamlined purchasing, connected suppliers, and reduced paperwork. That was the value proposition, and it worked.

Then the capability of AI shifted what was possible.

Over the past several years, we rebuilt the platform architecture to make AI central, not optional. Invoice processing that once required human review now runs at 99% accuracy with 90% straight-through processing. Budgeting that required month-end reconciliation now shows live GP% commitment before invoices even arrive. Spend analytics that required a finance analyst to compile now surface automatically across properties.

The hospitality domain knowledge did not change. The speed and intelligence of the platform did.
