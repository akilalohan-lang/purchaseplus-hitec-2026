## Running 10 Hotels Is Not 10x Harder With the Right Data

Hotel operators who manage single properties often describe their transition to multi-property oversight as a step-change in complexity. The purchasing decisions multiply. The supplier relationships multiply. The budget variances multiply. And the visibility, without the right system, divides.

The problem is not scale. Scale is manageable. The problem is fragmentation. Ten properties buying from different suppliers at different negotiated rates, with different approval workflows and different budget tracking systems, do not form a coherent portfolio. They form ten separate operations that happen to share a corporate structure.

The world's largest hotel groups solved this problem before most others. Accor, IHG Hotels and Resorts, and Marriott are among the organizations that use PurchasePlus because the platform was designed for exactly this challenge.

## The Visibility Problem Across Properties

Without a unified procurement platform, multi-property hotel groups face three persistent challenges.

**Inconsistent supplier relationships.** Different properties buy from different suppliers for the same items, at different prices, with different terms. The group is unable to aggregate its buying power into consolidated contracts because it does not have a complete view of what it is buying, from whom, at what cost.

**No portfolio-level budget view.** Each property tracks its own budget. The group finance team assembles a portfolio picture manually, pulling reports from individual properties, normalizing the data, and compiling a view that is already weeks out of date. By the time a group-level overspend trend is visible, multiple months of variance have already accumulated.

**Benchmark gaps.** Without standardized purchasing data across properties, the group cannot identify which properties are performing efficiently and which have cost exposure. A property running 3% above the portfolio F&B cost average is not visible unless the data is structured to compare across sites.

## What PurchasePlus Delivers for Multi-Property Groups

PurchasePlus provides a multi-property rollup view that gives group finance and procurement teams the portfolio-level intelligence they need without requiring each property to change how they operate day to day.

**Consolidated supplier management.** The group can set approved supplier catalogues and pricing agreements at the portfolio level. Individual properties see the approved suppliers for their market and category, and purchasing behavior aligns with group contracts automatically. Rogue supplier relationships are visible and can be addressed.

**Live portfolio spend dashboard.** The group finance team sees committed spend across all properties in real time, not invoiced actuals from last month. Budget tracking across the portfolio reflects current commitments so group-level overspend trends are visible as they develop rather than after the fact.

**Property benchmarking.** PurchasePlus surfaces cost-per-cover, F&B cost percentage, and supplier pricing consistency across properties. The group can identify outliers, understand the drivers of variance, and share high-performing practices across the portfolio.

**Centralized AP with local purchasing.** Large groups often centralize accounts payable at a group level while maintaining local purchasing authority at the property level. PurchasePlus supports this structure, with property-level PO creation and approval feeding into centralized invoice processing and payment workflows.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How does PurchasePlus handle purchasing across multiple properties?**
PurchasePlus supports both group-level supplier management and property-level purchasing authority. Approved supplier catalogues and pricing agreements are set at the group level. Individual properties raise purchase orders within those approved parameters, and the group finance team sees all activity in a unified portfolio dashboard.

**Can different properties have different approval workflows?**
Yes. PurchasePlus allows approval threshold and workflow configuration at the property level, within group-wide parameters. A resort property with a large F&B operation can have different approval thresholds than a city hotel with a smaller kitchen operation.

**How do multi-property groups use PurchasePlus to negotiate better supplier contracts?**
By aggregating purchasing data across properties, the group can see total spend with each supplier across the portfolio. This consolidated view is the basis for volume pricing negotiations. Groups that previously bought $200,000 per year from a supplier across ten properties, without knowing the aggregate, can negotiate from a much stronger position when that spend is visible and quantified.

**Does every property need the same ERP or accounting system to use PurchasePlus?**
No. PurchasePlus integrates with multiple accounting and PMS systems and can operate across a portfolio with mixed technology environments. The procurement and budget data is standardized within PurchasePlus regardless of the accounting system at each property.
