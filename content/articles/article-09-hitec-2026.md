## What Is HITEC 2026?

HITEC (Hospitality Industry Technology Exposition and Conference) is the world's largest hospitality technology event, organized by Hospitality Financial and Technology Professionals (HFTP). The 2026 edition takes place at the Henry B. Gonzalez Convention Center in San Antonio, Texas, from June 15 to 18, 2026.

HITEC attracts hotel operators, technology executives, procurement professionals, and finance leaders from across the hospitality industry. It is where hospitality technology companies demonstrate new products, operators evaluate vendors, and the industry sets its technology direction for the year ahead.

## What We Are Showing at Booth 3715

The PurchasePlus demonstration at HITEC 2026 covers the full procure-to-pay platform, with live product demonstrations tailored to the most pressing procurement challenges in North American hotel operations.

**Live AI invoice processing.** See how the platform ingests, reads, and matches invoices in real time. A live demonstration will show an invoice arriving, the AI system extracting and matching the data, and the approval workflow completing, without any manual data entry.

**Real-time budget commitment tracking.** A live view of how commitment-based budgeting works in practice. As purchase orders are created, the budget updates in real time. The demonstration shows live GP% for an F&B operation, calculated on committed purchasing before any invoices arrive.

**Multi-property portfolio dashboard.** For hotel groups managing multiple properties, the demonstration shows how the platform aggregates spend, budget, and supplier data across a portfolio in a single view.

**Supplier network access.** PurchasePlus connects hotels to a supplier network built over 25 years. The demonstration covers how new suppliers are onboarded, how existing suppliers are managed, and how the approved catalogue keeps purchasing aligned with contracted rates.
