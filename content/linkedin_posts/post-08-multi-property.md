Ten hotels. One screen. Every invoice, every supplier, every cost.

This is what PurchasePlus looks like for a multi-property hotel group.

Most hotel groups grow faster than their procurement infrastructure. The first property has a system that works. The second property does something slightly different. By the tenth property, the group has ten different supplier relationships for the same categories, ten different approval workflows, and a finance team compiling the portfolio picture manually from ten different reports.

The buying power is there. The visibility is not.

PurchasePlus gives the group a single platform where all purchasing activity, from all properties, is visible in real time. Group supplier contracts flow to every property automatically. Budget performance across the portfolio is visible before month-end. An outlier property running 4 points above the group F&B cost average surfaces immediately, not in next month's board pack.

And the buying power the group has always had? Now the numbers are in one place to prove it in a supplier negotiation.
