# POST 06: Live GP% / Commitment-Based Budgeting
**SCHEDULE:** Tuesday June 9, 2026
**TONE:** Challenge-the-status-quo, finance leader audience

---

It should not take until month-end to know if you are over budget.

In most hotels, that is exactly how it works. The kitchen places orders throughout the month. Invoices arrive weeks later. The finance team reconciles the numbers. Month-end arrives and the F&B cost percentage is 3 points higher than budgeted.

By the time you can see it, it is already done.

PurchasePlus shows live GP% as orders are placed. Not at month-end. Not at week-end. As the commitment is made.

When a chef orders a case of beef tenderloin for Thursday's function, that cost is immediately reflected in the F&B budget. The live GP% updates. If it moves outside tolerance, the finance team sees it that day, not three weeks later.

Commitment-based budgeting does not change how the kitchen works. It changes when the finance team can see what the kitchen is committing to.

That gap, between when the decision is made and when it becomes visible, is where most hotel budget overruns live.

We can close it.
