Use these as the substance behind booth conversations and the deck.

**AI Invoicing**
- 99%+ accuracy on line-item extraction
- 90% straight-through processing rate (no manual rekeying)
- 75% reduction in invoice processing costs
- 1 FTE saved per 2,000 monthly invoices

**Purchasing and control**
- Commitment-based budgeting: teams see their position before the invoice arrives, not 30 days after
- Policy violations blocked at the PO stage, before money moves
- Multi-property rollup: compare F&B performance across 10+ properties in one view
- ERP-agnostic: Xero, NetSuite, Dynamics 365, MYOB, auto GL mapping

**Real-time GP visibility**
- Live gross profit percentage across the full portfolio
- Spend by department: F&B, Rooms, Events, Admin, Services
- Rebate tracking by property in real time
